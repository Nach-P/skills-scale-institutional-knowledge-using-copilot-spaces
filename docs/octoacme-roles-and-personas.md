# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interactions
- Work with Product Managers to clarify customer outcomes and acceptance criteria.
- Collaborate with Project Managers on estimates, progress, dependencies, and delivery risks.
- Partner with QA/Test Engineers, Design/UX roles, and Technical Leads to deliver testable, usable, maintainable solutions.

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interactions
- Partner with the Product Lead on strategy and priority decisions.
- Work with Developers and the Technical Lead on feasibility, estimates, and trade-offs.
- Collaborate with Design/UX roles on user needs and QA/Test Engineers on acceptance criteria.

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interactions
- Align scope and milestones with the Product Manager and Product Lead.
- Coordinate delivery risks and dependencies with Developers and Technical Leads.
- Prepare launch communications and operational handoffs with QA/Test Engineers and Support/Operations.
- Escalate business-impacting issues to Sponsors and engage stakeholder groups for decisions.

---

## Product Leads

### Role Summary
Product Leads own product direction and set strategic priorities. They help translate customer and business needs into clear outcomes for the product team.

### Responsibilities
- Define and communicate product vision and strategic priorities
- Resolve significant product trade-offs and value-versus-risk decisions
- Align stakeholders on outcomes and success measures
- Support prioritization and roadmap decisions

### Goals
- Maintain a clear, outcome-focused product direction
- Align product investments with customer and business value
- Improve decision speed and cross-team alignment

### Typical Communication
- Strategic planning sessions and roadmap reviews
- Executive and stakeholder briefings
- Escalation discussions with Product Managers, Project Managers, and Sponsors

### Interactions
- Partner with Product Managers on outcomes, backlog trade-offs, and prioritization.
- Align with Project Managers on scope, milestones, resource constraints, and escalations.
- Work with Sponsors on strategic decisions and business-impacting issues.
- Consult Design/UX, Technical Leads, and QA/Test Engineers on strategy implications and delivery readiness.

---

## QA/Test Engineers

### Role Summary
QA/Test Engineers define and execute testing strategies that validate quality, requirements, and release readiness.

### Responsibilities
- Define test approaches and acceptance validation
- Execute automated and manual testing as appropriate
- Report defects and track quality risks
- Support smoke testing, regression testing, and release verification

### Goals
- Prevent defects and identify quality risks early
- Ensure solutions meet acceptance criteria
- Improve repeatable, observable release validation

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and test readiness reviews
- Defect reports and quality status updates
- Release and go/no-go checks

### Interactions
- Work with Developers to build testable implementations and resolve defects.
- Collaborate with Product Managers and Product Leads on acceptance criteria and release decisions.
- Partner with Design/UX roles on usability validation and Support/Operations on operational readiness.

---

## Design/UX Researchers

### Role Summary
Design/UX Researchers identify user needs and shape usable, accessible solutions through research and interaction design.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability studies
- Develop and test interaction, workflow, and experience designs
- Document accessibility and design requirements
- Share evidence and insights that guide product decisions

### Goals
- Improve usability and accessibility
- Reduce assumptions by grounding decisions in user evidence
- Create coherent experiences across the product

### Typical Communication
- Research readouts and design reviews
- User feedback and experiment findings
- Acceptance criteria and design specifications

### Interactions
- Partner with Product Managers on user needs, outcomes, and prioritization.
- Collaborate with Developers on feasible implementation and rapid validation.
- Work with QA/Test Engineers on usability and acceptance considerations.
- Share insights with Project Managers and stakeholders through planning and review sessions.

---

## Technical Leads/Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects guide technical direction, quality standards, system design, and significant technical risks.

### Responsibilities
- Lead architecture and technical design decisions
- Establish engineering quality and maintainability standards
- Identify technical dependencies, risks, and mitigation options
- Support estimation and sequencing of complex work

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, secure, maintainable solutions
- Reduce technical uncertainty and avoidable rework
- Improve engineering consistency and observability

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture decisions
- Planning sessions and dependency reviews
- Risk assessments and engineering guidance

### Interactions
- Coach and align Developers on technical implementation and standards.
- Partner with Product Managers on feasibility, scope, and trade-offs.
- Work with Project Managers to identify technical dependencies, estimates, and schedule risks.
- Collaborate with QA/Test Engineers and Support/Operations on testability, reliability, monitoring, and incident readiness.

---

## Sponsors

### Role Summary
Sponsors provide strategic authority, resources, and executive decisions for the project. They become especially important when progress requires business-level action or escalation.

### Responsibilities
- Define or endorse the project's strategic importance and outcomes
- Secure resources and remove cross-organizational barriers
- Approve major scope, budget, or priority changes
- Support resolution of business-impacting escalations

### Goals
- Ensure the project advances organizational objectives
- Provide timely executive decisions and sponsorship
- Balance strategic value, resources, and risk

### Typical Communication
- Milestone and sponsor reviews
- Executive status updates
- High-level risk and decision escalations

### Interactions
- Align with Product Leads on strategic direction and outcomes.
- Receive project status and escalation recommendations from Project Managers.
- Work with Product Managers on major priority or scope decisions.
- Engage stakeholder groups and dependent leaders when organizational action is required.

---

## Support/Operations/Site Reliability Engineers

### Role Summary
Support and Operations or Site Reliability Engineers prepare systems and teams for production, coordinate operational handoffs, and improve reliability and incident response.

### Responsibilities
- Plan operational readiness and support handoffs
- Establish monitoring, alerting, backups, and recovery procedures
- Prepare incident response, escalation, and on-call processes
- Analyze operational issues and identify reliability improvements

### Goals
- Improve service reliability and recovery
- Make releases observable and supportable
- Reduce operational uncertainty and incident impact

### Typical Communication
- Operational readiness and release reviews
- Monitoring and incident dashboards
- On-call and incident follow-up reports

### Interactions
- Coordinate launch planning and operational timing with Project Managers.
- Work with Developers and Technical Leads on observability, deployment, and reliability risks.
- Partner with QA/Test Engineers on smoke testing, monitoring validation, and rollback readiness.
- Provide handoff information to support teams and contribute to blameless retrospectives.

---

## How roles work together

Roles may be combined or adapted for smaller teams, but decision boundaries and handoffs should remain clear. Cross-role accountability is strongest when each role:

- receives a clear request or decision from the role that owns the prior step,
- identifies dependencies and risks early, and
- confirms acceptance or handoff through project records and status updates.

Team-level delivery starts with the Project Manager coordinating plans and the Product Manager or Product Lead defining outcomes and priorities. Developers and Technical Leads implement solutions, QA/Test Engineers validate them, and Design/UX roles ensure that user needs are represented. Sponsors provide strategic and resource decisions when escalated. Support/Operations roles ensure readiness and operational continuity throughout release and incident lifecycles.
